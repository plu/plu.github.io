# HALT!

HALT! is a calm, simple way to practise bilateral stimulation on your own. A dot moves slowly from
one side of the screen to the other; you follow it with your eyes while keeping your head still.
Each set stops by itself, so you can take a breath and notice how you feel before starting the next.

HALT! runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.
