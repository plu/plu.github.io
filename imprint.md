This website respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy notice explains how I collect, use, and protect your information when you visit my website.

### Analytics

This website uses **Plausible Analytics**, a privacy-focused web analytics service. Plausible is self-hosted and designed to be fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations.

**What Plausible collects:**

- Page views and unique visitors
- Referrer information (where visitors come from)
- Device information (browser, operating system, device type)
- Geographic location (country and city level only)

**What Plausible does NOT collect:**

- Personal information or personally identifiable information
- Cookies or persistent identifiers
- Cross-site or cross-device tracking
- IP addresses (not stored)

All analytics data is anonymized and aggregated. No personal data is collected or stored that could identify individual visitors.

### Data processing legal basis

The processing of analytics data is based on my legitimate interest in analyzing website traffic and improving user experience (Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR).

### Data storage and retention

Analytics data is stored on my self-hosted Plausible instance and retained for 24 months. After this period, data is automatically deleted.
