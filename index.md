## About me

I'm a software engineer with over 20+ years of experience, currently working at **Kleinanzeigen** (formerly eBay Kleinanzeigen) where I help build and maintain Germany's most popular classifieds platform.

My journey in software development has taken me from backend systems to mobile applications, with a recent focus on iOS development. I've been working remotely for more than 9 years, long before it became mainstream.

## Side Projects

### Less Paper iOS App

<div class="project-section">
  <img src="/assets/less-paper.webp" alt="Less Paper App Icon" class="app-icon" />
  <div class="project-content">
    <a href="https://apps.apple.com/de/app/less-paper/id6464425056?l=en-GB">Less Paper</a> is a side project I work on in my spare time - a native iOS client for the <a href="https://github.com/paperless-ngx/paperless-ngx">paperless-ngx</a> project. Paperless-ngx is a community-developed supercharged version of paperless: a machine learning powered document management system that automatically organizes and indexes your physical documents into a searchable online archive. As a bit of a home automation geek, I wanted a polished mobile experience to access my document archive on the go, so I built this native iOS app to provide seamless integration with my self-hosted paperless-ngx instance.
  </div>
</div>
